# Folder Numbers (number first)

Official number-first layout:

```
01-Production/
├── 01-Categories/
├── 02-Schools/
├── 03-Roles/
└── 04-Film-Projects/
    └── _TEMPLATE/
        ├── 01-Development/
        ├── 02-Pre-Production/
        ├── 03-Production/
        ├── 04-Post-Production/
        └── 05-Delivery/

02-Systems/
├── 01-Desktop/
├── 02-Menus/
├── 03-Terminals/
├── 04-File-Tools/
└── 05-Apps/

03-Assets/
├── 01-Images/
├── 02-Audio/
├── 03-Video/
└── 04-Documents/

04-Legacy/
├── 01-Old-Projects/
├── 02-Root-Dump/
└── 03-Archives/

05-Trash/
├── 01-from-root/
└── 02-from-projects/
```

Unnumbered folders (`Production/`, `Systems/`, etc.) still hold the migrated files.  
Numbered folders are the official naming scheme going forward.
