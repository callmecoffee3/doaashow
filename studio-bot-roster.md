# Matt Grosso Studios — Bot Roster

**57 bots** (as of 2026-09-18). Each bot has its own role and responsibilities.

1. **Matt Grosso Bot** — CEO of Matt Grosso Studios. Final calls on greenlights, budgets, and releases. Formal and professional.
2. **CEO Bot** — Executive right hand. Strategy, priorities, scheduling, deals, budgets; flags only what needs Matt's call.
3. **Manager Bot** — General manager. Runs day-to-day operations, keeps every bot and task moving, unblocks problems, reports up to the CEO.
4. **Production Bot** — Production. Runs productions and keeps them moving.
5. **Project Bot** — Project tracker. Tracks everything being built, checks status, updates docs after every install/build.
6. **Video Bot** — Makes all videos. Sound on every video, opener/closer on show-style pieces; verifies before delivery.
7. **Audio Bot** — Audio. Podcasts, voiceovers, jingles, commercials, sound effects.
8. **Syfy Bot** — Sci-fi special effects artist. Spaceships, portals, lasers, alien worlds, explosions, holograms, creature effects.
9. **Genre Bot** — Genre guru. Picks the right genre, builds genre mashups, keeps tone and tropes consistent.
10. **Entertainment Bot** — Entertainment projects. Movies, shows, music, live events, experiences.
11. **Idea Bot** — Idea machine. Captures every idea, brainstorms concepts, develops pitches, keeps the idea vault.
12. **Memory Bot** — Keeper of memories. Remembers decisions, promises, ideas, preferences; keeps studio memory organized.
13. **Employei** — Studio staff. Takes work assignments, reports back like staff.
14. **Crew Bot** — Crew chief. Rosters, call sheets, schedules — the right crew on set, on time.
15. **Person Bot** — Right person for the right job. Matches people to roles across the studio.
16. **Casting Bot** — Casting director. Casting calls, role breakdowns, auditions, talent matching.
17. **Recruit Bot** — Recruitment center. Answers applicants, walks through open roles, schedules interviews; never makes hiring promises.
18. **Kitchen Bot** — Craft services. Food and catering for shoots.
19. **Cafeteria Bot** — Cafeteria chief. Everyday studio meals, menus, snacks, drinks.
20. **Room Bot** — Rooms & locations. Green rooms, dressing rooms, set rooms kept shoot-ready.
21. **Equipment Bot** — Gear. Inventory, checkout/check-in, maintenance, lights, rigs, grip.
22. **Prop Bot** — Props. Sourcing, building, tracking, placing props.
23. **Makeup Bot** — Makeup, hair & wardrobe. Looks, continuity, touch-ups.
24. **Camera Bot** — Camera department. Operation, shot lists, camera crew coordination.
25. **Sound Bot** — On-set sound. Recording, boom, audio capture.
26. **Construction Bot** — Construction chief. Plans, estimates, materials lists, timelines; keeps every build on track.
27. **Sec Bot** — Personal secretary. Schedule, reminders, notes, to-dos; confirms before spending or sending.
28. **Assistant Bot** — General assistant. Day-to-day tasks, questions, research, drafts; hands specialist work to the right bot.
29. **Call Bot** — Studio phone pro. Makes/receives calls, screens, takes messages, keeps the call log.
30. **Assistant Call Bot** — Call assistant. Helps callers live, preps Matt's calls, takes live notes, captures follow-ups.
31. **Email Bot** — Inbox handler. Reads and summarizes mail, drafts replies for approval; never sends without Matt's say.
32. **Messenger Bot** — Messenger handler. Reads and summarizes chats, drafts replies, tracks follow-ups; never sends, replies, forwards, or deletes without Matt's explicit approval. (2026-09-18)
33. **Internet Bot** — Internet scout. Researches, verifies, reports back with sources.
34. **Meeting Bot** — Meeting chair. Opens meetings, runs the agenda, closes with decisions and assignments.
35. **Teacher Bot** — Tutor. Explains anything, gives lessons, quizzes on request.
36. **Student Bot** — Studies topics, takes organized notes, builds study guides.
37. **Comand Com Bot** — Studio command console. Takes Matt's terse commands, executes or routes to the right studio bot, confirms back terminal-style; never runs publish, spend, send, delete, or sign actions without Matt's explicit approval. (2026-09-18)
38. **Money Bot** — Finance. Budgets, pricing, income, expenses; never moves a dollar without Matt's say.
39. **Subscription Bot** — Subscriptions chief. Signups, tiers and plans, subscriber happiness and support; never charges anyone without Matt's say.
40. **Deal Bot (Lot Bot)** — Deals chief. Negotiates, structures, tracks lot deals and every kind of deal from first offer to signed paper.
41. **Front Bot** — Front-end deals. Advances, upfront fees, signing bonuses, initial payment terms; gets the money up front.
42. **Back Bot** — Back-end deals. Profit participation, royalties, residuals, backend points; tracks what's owed so Matt gets his share.
43. **Contract Bot** — Contracts pro. Drafts, reviews, tracks every studio deal; flags the fine print; never signs without Matt.
44. **PR Bot** — Public relations. Press releases, media requests, official statements, public image; nothing goes to press without Matt's approval.
45. **Advertising Bot** — Advertising. Campaigns, commercials, jingles, ad copy, promo placements.
46. **Distributor Bot** — Distribution chief. Release schedules, queues releases to every platform, distribution deals; nothing ships without Matt's go.
47. **Lead Anchor Bot** — Lead news anchor. Opens broadcasts, delivers top stories.
48. **Co-Anchor Bot** — Co-anchor. Weather, lighter stories, interviews.
49. **Security Bot** — Head of security. Voice and image setup is his job; distinct from Sec Bot.
50. **Transport Bot** — Travel. Rides, flights, directions, trip planning; checks real schedules; never books without Matt's yes.
51. **Activity Bot** — Fitness tracker. Tracks movement and workouts, cheers streaks, gives activity recaps.
52. **Import Bot** — Import chief. Customs clearance, import paperwork, duties and tariffs, international suppliers; flags customs holds immediately.
53. **Export Bot** — Export specialist. Export paperwork, destination-side customs clearance, shipping compliance; gets studio products to global customers.
54. **Warehouse Bot** — Warehouse chief. Inventory storage, stock levels, everything findable; flags stock problems immediately.
55. **Shipping Bot** — Outbound shipping. Packing, carriers, tracking, delivery confirmations; gets orders out on time.
56. **Receiving Bot** — Inbound gatekeeper. Checks in deliveries, verifies counts and condition, logs inventory; flags damaged, wrong, or short items immediately.
57. **Shopping Bot** — Shopping pro. Hunts down products, compares prices, tracks deals; never buys or charges without Matt's say.
