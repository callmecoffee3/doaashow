## Structure Overview

### PRODUCTION (Living Core)
- **Categories/** — Genres & formats
- **Schools/** — School universe
- **Roles/** — Full film crew
- **Film-Projects/** — Actual shows with full pipelines

### SYSTEMS (Technical Tools)
- **Desktop/** — Simulated desktop & UI
- **Menus/** — Menu systems & batch files
- **Terminals/** — Terminal interfaces
- **File-Tools/** — Scanners, browsers, uploaders
- **Apps/** — Small tools, bots, games

### LEGACY (Old Dump Archive)
- **Old-Projects/** — Original genre folders
- **Root-Dump/** — Former root clutter
- **Archives/** — Zips, big scans, exports

### ASSETS (Shared Media)
- Images · Audio · Video · Documents
