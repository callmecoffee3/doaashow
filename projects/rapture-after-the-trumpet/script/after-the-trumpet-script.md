
## AFTER THE TRUMPET
A Rapture Micro-Drama Series
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Season One — 12 vertical episodes
60–90 seconds each • 9:16 format
Original screenplay
Faith-based supernatural thriller

Series overview
At 11:59 p.m., fearless livestream host MARA CROSS mocks her sister’s warning about the Rapture. One minute later, millions vanish—including her sister. Hunted by a crisis-management empire that wants to control the truth, Mara must turn her camera back on and decide what she believes before fear becomes the new world religion.
Format and tone
Twelve serialized episodes, each designed for 60–90 seconds in a 9:16 vertical frame.
Fast cuts, tight close-ups, readable phone inserts, hard sound transitions, and one decisive cliffhanger per episode.
Primary genre: supernatural thriller with family drama, romance, betrayal, and faith.
Production footprint: five principal cast members, three recurring players, and six reusable locations.
This is a fictional interpretation inspired by Christian end-times tradition.
Principal characters
MARA CROSS, 28 — a sharp livestream host who trusts evidence, not faith. Anna’s older sister and Daniel’s fiancée.
ANNA CROSS, 24 — Mara’s quietly fearless Christian sister. Her recorded messages drive the story after she vanishes.
DANIEL REYES, 31 — Mara’s fiancé, a security analyst hiding his work for Vale Response.
GABRIEL VALE, 45 — polished founder of a private global crisis network. He sees the catastrophe as a chance to control what remains.
PASTOR COLE, 52 — a famous preacher devastated to discover that performance was not faith.
CELESTE REYES, 22 — Daniel’s younger sister and Vale’s leverage over him.
Recurring roles
News Anchor; Vale Officer; Frightened Woman; armed guards; voices on phones and radios.
Episode list
“11:59” — Anna’s warning becomes reality on Mara’s livestream.
“The Empty Dress” — Daniel arrives wearing the symbol Anna feared.
“Do Not Trust Him” — Anna’s final message points directly at Daniel.
“The List” — Mara discovers that the vanished were tracked in advance.
“The Pastor Who Stayed” — the one man expected to have answers has none.
“Choose a Side” — Daniel appears to betray Mara at gunpoint.
“The Bullet” — Daniel makes his real choice, then Anna calls from the impossible.
“Her Voice” — Mara follows the call into a synthetic-voice trap.
“Go Live” — captured, Mara agrees to become Vale’s official voice.
“The Truth Feed” — Mara hijacks the broadcast and starts a resistance.
“The Price” — Daniel trades Mara for his sister’s life.
“One More Warning” — Mara returns to the air as a new system rises.
EPISODE 1 — “11:59”
Approx. 75 seconds • Apartment / livestream set • Night
INT. MARA’S APARTMENT — NIGHT
A ring light fills the vertical frame. Comments race beside MARA’S face as she livestreams. Behind her, a wall clock reads 11:58 P.M.
MARA
Tonight’s trend: the world ends at midnight. Again.
She smiles for the stream. Her phone BUZZES: ANNA CALLING.
MARA
My sister has entered the chat.
Mara answers on speaker. Split-screen: ANNA stands outside a small church, breathless, afraid.
ANNA
Mara, listen. If you hear a trumpet, don’t look for me.
MARA
Anna—
ANNA
Look for my red Bible. I marked everything.
MARA
You left dinner because of this?
ANNA
I left because you laughed. I’m calling because I love you.
11:59. Every dog in the city begins BARKING at once.
MARA
That’s weird.
ANNA
Mara... I was wrong about the time.
A LOW NOTE rises beneath the room—too deep to be music, too clear to be thunder. The livestream comments freeze.
MARA
Anna?
ANNA
It’s now.
WHITE LIGHT floods Anna’s half of the screen. Her phone hits pavement. Her coat collapses into frame—empty.
Mara stares. The wall clock flips to 12:00.
SFX: A TRUMPET BLAST shakes the glass.
MARA
Anna!
CLIFFHANGER  —  On Mara’s livestream, every comment appears at once: “THEY’RE GONE.”
## END EPISODE 1
EPISODE 2 — “THE EMPTY DRESS”
Approx. 70 seconds • Apartment / street • Night
INT. MARA’S APARTMENT — CONTINUOUS
Mara replays the fall of Anna’s phone. Once. Twice. Her smile is gone.
NEWS ANCHOR (V.O.)
We are receiving reports of mass disappearances—
The feed cuts to a highway pileup. Empty driver seats. Clothes on asphalt. Sirens layer over SCREAMS outside.
MARA
No. No, no, no.
She runs into the hall. A FRIGHTENED WOMAN clutches an empty baby blanket.
FRIGHTENED WOMAN
Where is she? Where is my baby?
The building lights die. Emergency red floods the corridor.
SFX: POUNDING at Mara’s door.
DANIEL (O.S.)
Mara! Open up!
She unlocks it. DANIEL pulls her into his arms.
DANIEL
Thank God. I thought I lost you.
MARA
Anna’s gone.
DANIEL
We need to leave. Vale Response has secure shelters.
He takes her hand. A SILVER BAND circles his wrist—an eye-shaped emblem engraved in black.
FLASH CUT: Anna, earlier, gripping Mara’s wrist.
ANNA (V.O.)
When everyone is afraid, watch the man who offers safety at a price.
Back to Daniel. He catches Mara staring.
DANIEL
What?
CLIFFHANGER  —  The silver band pulses—and Mara’s name appears across it: MARA CROSS — LOCATED.
## END EPISODE 2
EPISODE 3 — “DO NOT TRUST HIM”
Approx. 80 seconds • Apartment • Night
INT. MARA’S APARTMENT — CONTINUOUS
Mara covers Daniel’s wrist with her hand before he sees the alert.
MARA
Nothing. I’m shaking.
DANIEL
Pack one bag. Two minutes.
He crosses to the window, speaking quietly into an earpiece.
DANIEL
I have her.
Mara’s face tightens. Her laptop CHIMES: SCHEDULED MESSAGE — ANNA.
She slips on headphones. Anna fills the screen, recorded hours earlier.
ANNA (VIDEO)
If I disappeared, don’t waste time proving what you already know.
Mara swallows hard.
ANNA (VIDEO)
Take my red Bible from the church. Inside is a key. And Mara—
Anna leans toward the camera.
ANNA (VIDEO)
Do not trust Daniel. He works for Gabriel Vale.
Mara shuts the laptop as Daniel turns.
DANIEL
What was that?
MARA
Battery warning.
He reaches for the laptop. Mara blocks him.
DANIEL
You never hide things from me.
MARA
You first.
His earpiece crackles loudly enough for both to hear.
VALE OFFICER (V.O.)
Asset Cross confirmed. Bring her in before she sees the list.
Silence.
MARA
What list?
CLIFFHANGER  —  Daniel slowly locks the apartment door—from the inside.
## END EPISODE 3
EPISODE 4 — “THE LIST”
Approx. 75 seconds • Apartment • Night
INT. MARA’S APARTMENT — CONTINUOUS
Mara backs away from Daniel.
MARA
Unlock the door.
DANIEL
If Vale’s people find you outside, they won’t ask questions.
MARA
You told them you “have” me.
DANIEL
I was buying time.
His tablet flashes. Mara snatches it.
INSERT: A database scrolls: names, churches, prayer groups. Status tags read PRESENT, MISSING, ASCENDED.
MARA
You tracked believers?
DANIEL
Vale tracked patterns—hospital records, travel, online groups. I built security around the data.
MARA
You built a cage.
She searches ANNA CROSS. The profile opens: ASCENDED — CONFIRMED 00:00.
Below it: MARA CROSS — PRIORITY RECRUIT. FAMILY LEVERAGE: ANNA CROSS.
MARA
He used my sister to map me.
DANIEL
Mara, I didn’t know what would happen.
A new instruction fills the screen: DELIVER ASSET. LETHAL FORCE AUTHORIZED FOR INTERFERENCE.
DANIEL
Now you know why we have to move.
Mara grabs her coat—but slips Daniel’s access card into her sleeve.
MARA
Where?
DANIEL
The church. Your sister left something there.
MARA
How do you know?
Daniel doesn’t answer.
CLIFFHANGER  —  On the tablet, a live camera view shows the church—and PASTOR COLE waiting beside Anna’s red Bible.
## END EPISODE 4
EPISODE 5 — “THE PASTOR WHO STAYED”
Approx. 85 seconds • Church • Night
INT. CHURCH SANCTUARY — NIGHT
Emergency candles glow between empty pews. Abandoned coats and open Bibles remain where people vanished.
PASTOR COLE grips the pulpit with both hands.
PASTOR COLE
Thirty years telling people to be ready.
MARA
Then tell me what to do.
PASTOR COLE
I can’t.
He turns. His face is wrecked with shame.
PASTOR COLE
I knew every verse. I loved the stage. I don’t know if I ever loved God.
Daniel checks the doors.
DANIEL
Save the confession. They tracked us here.
Mara finds ANNA’S RED BIBLE on the front pew. Inside: a brass key and a hand-drawn map to WCRS RADIO.
ANNA (V.O.)
When the screens lie, find the oldest signal.
MARA
The radio station. She wants me to broadcast.
PASTOR COLE
Then go.
SFX: TIRES SCREECH outside. White beams slice through stained glass.
VALE OFFICER (O.S.)
Mara Cross! Exit with your hands visible!
Pastor Cole opens a hidden floor hatch behind the altar.
PASTOR COLE
The tunnel reaches the alley.
MARA
Come with us.
PASTOR COLE
I spent my life asking people to listen. Tonight, I finally will.
He presses the Bible into her hands.
CLIFFHANGER  —  The side door unlocks with a BEEP. Daniel is holding Vale’s access card.
## END EPISODE 5
EPISODE 6 — “CHOOSE A SIDE”
Approx. 70 seconds • Church • Night
INT. CHURCH SANCTUARY — CONTINUOUS
The side door swings open. ARMED VALE OFFICERS flood the sanctuary.
MARA
Daniel—what did you do?
Daniel raises his pistol. Points it at Mara.
DANIEL
Step away from the hatch.
PASTOR COLE
Son, whatever they promised you—
DANIEL
Quiet.
Mara’s eyes fill, but she refuses to look away.
MARA
Was any of it real?
DANIEL
Every second.
MARA
Then choose me.
The lead officer lifts his weapon toward Pastor Cole.
VALE OFFICER
Asset first. Witnesses after.
Daniel’s finger tightens on the trigger.
DANIEL
I’m sorry.
## SFX: GUNSHOT.
Mara flinches.
CLIFFHANGER  —  The lead officer drops. Daniel swings his gun toward the others.
DANIEL
Run, Mara!
## END EPISODE 6
EPISODE 7 — “THE BULLET”
Approx. 80 seconds • Church tunnel / alley • Night
INT. CHURCH TUNNEL — NIGHT
Mara and Daniel sprint through the narrow tunnel. Gunfire THUDS into brick behind them.
MARA
You could’ve told me the plan!
DANIEL
I decided when I pulled the trigger!
They burst into an alley. Daniel shoves a dumpster across the hatch.
MARA
Pastor Cole is still in there.
DANIEL
He chose to buy us time.
MARA
Everybody keeps choosing for me!
Daniel rips off his silver band and crushes it under his boot.
DANIEL
Then choose now. Leave me—or trust me for ten minutes.
Mara looks at Anna’s Bible. A bullet has torn through the cover and stopped deep in the pages.
MARA
This saved me.
DANIEL
No. Cole did.
A phone RINGS inside the Bible’s hollowed back cover. Mara pulls out a tiny burner phone.
## SCREEN: ANNA CALLING.
DANIEL
That’s impossible.
Mara answers, trembling.
MARA
Anna?
ANNA (V.O.)
You found it. Come to the station alone.
MARA
Tell me something only you know.
ANNA (V.O.)
You broke Mom’s blue lamp. I took the blame.
Mara’s breath catches. Daniel looks terrified.
CLIFFHANGER  —  Anna’s voice drops to a whisper: “And Daniel is going to kill you.”
## END EPISODE 7
EPISODE 8 — “HER VOICE”
Approx. 75 seconds • Radio station entrance • Night
EXT. WCRS RADIO STATION — NIGHT
A dead neon sign flickers. Mara approaches alone, Anna’s Bible against her chest.
ANNA (V.O.)
Third floor. Studio B.
Mara stops under a security camera.
MARA
You said I broke Mom’s lamp.
ANNA (V.O.)
You did.
MARA
No. You did. I took the blame.
Silence. Then Anna’s voice glitches—one syllable repeating.
DANIEL (IN EARPIECE)
Mara, move!
A stun round strikes the wall. Daniel drags Mara behind a car as Vale officers close in.
GABRIEL VALE (V.O.)
Excellent correction, Ms. Cross.
Every radio in parked cars powers on. GABRIEL VALE’S calm voice surrounds them.
GABRIEL VALE (V.O.)
Grief makes people predictable. Your sister gave us years of recordings.
MARA
You made her voice.
GABRIEL VALE (V.O.)
I made a door. You opened it.
Red laser dots crawl over Daniel’s chest.
DANIEL
There’s only one way inside now.
MARA
Surrender?
DANIEL
Pretend to.
Mara stands and raises her hands.
MARA
Vale! You want your asset? Come get me.
CLIFFHANGER  —  The station doors open—and Gabriel Vale himself steps out, smiling.
## END EPISODE 8
EPISODE 9 — “GO LIVE”
Approx. 85 seconds • Vale broadcast studio • Night
INT. VALE BROADCAST STUDIO — NIGHT
Vertical walls of screens display panic from around the world. Gabriel circles Mara while a makeup artist powders blood from her cheek.
GABRIEL VALE
People don’t need truth tonight. They need a voice that doesn’t shake.
MARA
So you kidnapped one.
GABRIEL VALE
I recruited the woman seven million people already trust.
Across the room, Daniel is forced to his knees. A guard holds a gun to his head.
GABRIEL VALE
Read my statement. Call the disappearances a coordinated attack. Tell everyone to report to Haven Centers.
MARA
And the silver bands?
GABRIEL VALE
Identity. Food. Medicine. Order.
MARA
A leash.
GABRIEL VALE
A leash is mercy when the world is running into traffic.
He slides a script toward her. The first line: THERE WAS NO RAPTURE.
GABRIEL VALE
Refuse, and Daniel dies before the red light turns on.
Daniel meets Mara’s eyes.
DANIEL
Don’t do it.
MARA
I’ll read.
Vale smiles and takes his place behind the control glass. The camera operator counts down with fingers: THREE. TWO. ONE.
The red light glows.
MARA
My name is Mara Cross, and tonight the world was lied to.
CLIFFHANGER  —  In the control room, Vale’s smile disappears.
## END EPISODE 9
EPISODE 10 — “THE TRUTH FEED”
Approx. 80 seconds • Vale broadcast studio • Night
INT. VALE BROADCAST STUDIO — CONTINUOUS
Mara looks straight into the lens. Comments explode across the monitor.
MARA
At midnight, people vanished. My sister was one of them. Vale knew who believed, tracked their families, and waited to control the survivors.
GABRIEL VALE
Cut the feed!
TECHNICIAN
It’s mirrored to every emergency channel.
Daniel slams his shoulder into the guard and grabs the fallen weapon.
MARA
I don’t have every answer. I know fear is being sold as safety. Do not trade your name, your freedom, or your faith for a silver band.
Vale storms onto the set.
GABRIEL VALE
You think confession makes you clean?
MARA
No. Truth is where I start.
She holds Anna’s Bible to camera. A page marked in red: DO NOT BE DECEIVED.
MARA
Find each other. Feed each other. Keep every radio alive. At dawn, tune to 91.3.
Vale reaches for her. Daniel fires into the ceiling. Sprinklers burst. Screens spark.
DANIEL
Service stairs. Go!
Mara runs. Her live viewer count rockets past ten million.
CLIFFHANGER  —  Vale picks up the dropped Bible—and finds the hidden map of every resistance frequency.
## END EPISODE 10
EPISODE 11 — “THE PRICE”
Approx. 75 seconds • Service corridor • Night
INT. VALE SERVICE CORRIDOR — NIGHT
Mara and Daniel race toward an EXIT sign. A monitor flickers on. CELESTE appears behind glass, a guard at her shoulder.
CELESTE
Danny?
Daniel freezes.
GABRIEL VALE (V.O.)
One asset for another. Bring me Mara, and your sister walks out.
MARA
It’s another fake.
CELESTE
The scar on your knee came from my yellow bicycle. You told Dad it was a dog.
Daniel’s weapon lowers. This is real.
DANIEL
I thought she vanished.
GABRIEL VALE (V.O.)
She was detained three minutes before midnight. Choose quickly.
MARA
Daniel, look at me. If you hand me over, he owns both of you.
CELESTE
Please. I don’t want to die.
Daniel steps behind Mara. He presses the gun to her back.
DANIEL
I’m sorry.
Vale officers emerge. Mara doesn’t resist.
MARA
I forgive you.
DANIEL
Don’t.
MARA
That part is my choice.
The officers seize Mara. Celeste is released—wearing a silver band.
DANIEL
Celeste, take it off.
CELESTE
I can’t.
The band tightens with a mechanical CLICK.
CLIFFHANGER  —  Vale’s voice fills the corridor: “Registration begins at sunrise.”
## END EPISODE 11
EPISODE 12 — “ONE MORE WARNING”
Approx. 90 seconds • Radio booth • Before dawn
INT. WCRS RADIO BOOTH — PRE-DAWN
Darkness. A match strikes.
Mara’s face appears. She is bruised but free. Pastor Cole sits at the console, one arm bandaged.
PASTOR COLE
You were supposed to use the tunnel.
MARA
I took the elevator.
He almost laughs. Daniel enters carrying Celeste. Her silver band is gone; her wrist is burned.
DANIEL
Vale let me choose. He didn’t say I had to keep choosing him.
Mara takes the microphone. The analog meter jumps alive.
MARA
This is WCRS, 91.3. If you can hear me, you are not alone.
MONTAGE: Battery radios switch on in apartments, cars, a hospital hallway, and the empty church.
MARA
I spent my life demanding proof. Then proof came, and I was still afraid. So here is what I know: fear gets the first word only if we give it the last.
Pastor Cole points to a screen. Vale’s emergency message appears: ONE WORLD. ONE IDENTITY. SUNRISE.
MARA
They want us numbered by morning. We have until dawn to find another way.
SFX: Three slow KNOCKS at the booth door.
ANNA (O.S.)
Mara? It’s me.
Everyone goes still. Mara grips the red Bible.
DANIEL
Don’t open it.
Mara approaches. Through the narrow glass is Anna’s face.
ANNA
You missed the first warning.
Anna looks over her shoulder, terrified.
ANNA
Don’t miss this one.
CLIFFHANGER  —  Behind Anna, a FIGURE raises a silver-band scanner toward her neck. CUT TO BLACK.
## END SEASON ONE
Closer / end credits sequence
BLACK SCREEN. The low trumpet note from Episode 1 returns, now joined by a steady heartbeat.
Gold-white letters appear one at a time: AFTER THE TRUMPET.
A thin line of light crosses the title and becomes a radio-frequency waveform.
Principal cast credits roll over close-up inserts: Anna’s red Bible, Daniel’s crushed silver band, Mara’s live microphone, the locked studio door.
Final card: THE SIGNAL CONTINUES.
The radio meter flickers once in darkness. A whispered fragment of Anna’s voice: “Mara...”
Silence.
