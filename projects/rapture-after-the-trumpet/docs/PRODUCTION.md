# Production Notes — After the Trumpet

## Format specs

- Aspect ratio: 9:16 vertical (1080x1920)
- Episode length: 60–90 seconds
- Style: fast cuts, tight close-ups, readable phone inserts, hard sound transitions
- Each episode: one decisive cliffhanger

## Principal cast

- MARA CROSS, 28 — livestream host; trusts evidence, not faith
- ANNA CROSS, 24 — Mara's sister; vanishes in Ep 1, her recorded messages drive the story
- DANIEL REYES, 31 — Mara's fiancé; security analyst secretly working for Vale Response
- GABRIEL VALE, 45 — founder of a private global crisis network
- PASTOR COLE, 52 — famous preacher left behind
- CELESTE REYES, 22 — Daniel's younger sister; Vale's leverage over him

Recurring: News Anchor, Vale Officer, Frightened Woman, armed guards, phone/radio voices.

## Locations (six reusable)

Mara's livestream setup, the church, Vale Response staging area, broadcast studio, the dock/street exteriors, Pastor Cole's church office.

## Reusable assets (in `media/`)

- `opener.mp4` — 7s title sequence: AI-animated rapture key art, "RAPTURE / A Matt Grosso Studios Micro Drama", MGS bumper audio. Plays before every episode.
- `closer.mp4` — 7s end card: "TO BE CONTINUED / Made by Matt Grosso Studios". Plays after every episode.
- `ep1-teaser.mp4` — 44s promo cut for Episode 1 ("11:59"): opener + Mara livestream + Anna phone card + rapture light + aftermath + closer, with dialogue text cards and a synthesized tension bed.
- `keyart.webp` — series key art.

## Media specs

- Opener/closer: 1080x1920, H.264 + AAC, 24fps
- Ep 1 teaser: 1080x1920, H.264 + AAC, ~44s
