# Episode Guide — After the Trumpet, Season 1

Twelve episodes. Each one ends on a hard cliffhanger designed to pull the viewer into the next.

## Ep 1 — "11:59"
Anna's warning becomes reality on Mara's livestream. Mara mocks her sister's Rapture warning on air; at midnight, viewers' feeds go dark one by one — and Anna is gone.

## Ep 2 — "The Empty Dress"
Daniel arrives wearing the symbol Anna feared. Mara's fiancé shows up in a Vale Response jacket — the same crisis outfit Anna warned her about.

## Ep 3 — "Do Not Trust Him"
Anna's final message points directly at Daniel. A recorded message from Anna, left before she vanished, names Daniel as someone Mara can't trust.

## Ep 4 — "The List"
Mara discovers that the vanished were tracked in advance. A leaked Vale Response list shows the disappeared were catalogued before the event.

## Ep 5 — "The Pastor Who Stayed"
The one man expected to have answers has none. Pastor Cole — the famous preacher left behind — admits his faith was performance.

## Ep 6 — "Choose a Side"
Daniel appears to betray Mara at gunpoint. Vale officers close in; Daniel raises a weapon and Mara has to decide in a heartbeat.

## Ep 7 — "The Bullet"
Daniel makes his real choice, then Anna calls from the impossible. The gunshot isn't what it seemed — and Mara's phone rings with Anna's voice.

## Ep 8 — "Her Voice"
Mara follows the call into a synthetic-voice trap. The voice that sounds like Anna leads Mara somewhere she shouldn't go.

## Ep 9 — "Go Live"
Captured, Mara agrees to become Vale's official voice. Gabriel Vale offers her the biggest audience on earth — if she reads his script.

## Ep 10 — "The Truth Feed"
Mara hijacks the broadcast and starts a resistance. On live air, she goes off-script and tells the world what Vale is hiding.

## Ep 11 — "The Price"
Daniel trades Mara for his sister's life. Vale holds Celeste — Daniel's younger sister — and the exchange is set.

## Ep 12 — "One More Warning"
Mara returns to the air as a new system rises. Season finale: Mara broadcasts one last warning while Vale's new order takes shape. The signal continues.
