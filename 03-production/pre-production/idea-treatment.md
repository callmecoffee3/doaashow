# Idea Treatment

**Capture a raw idea and start shaping it into something you can develop.**

Use this when an idea first hits — before a full logline, synopsis, or treatment. Keep it loose, playful, and fast.

## 2. Free write / brain dump

Write without editing. Dump everything that came with the idea — images, feelings, lines, “what ifs,” references, vibes.

---

## 3. Why this idea?

- Why does it interest you right now?
- What emotion or experience does it chase?
- Who is it for (even if just you)?
