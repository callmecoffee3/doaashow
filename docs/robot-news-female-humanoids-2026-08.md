## Headline Updates (Late August 2026)

### 1X Technologies — X1 Neo Beta (Aug 30, 2026)
- Norwegian company **1X** unveiled the **X1 Neo Beta** in a short intro video.
- Designed for safe home interaction (including hugs).
- Upgrades from earlier A1: feet instead of wheels, expressive 10-fingered hands (replacing grippers), sleek expressionless black glass face (more human-like).
- Currently in limited home trials to gather safety and interaction data.
- Focus: soft, friendly companion robot for everyday home environments.

**Media:**
- Official-style intro / home demo videos available via 1X channels and coverage (search “1X Neo Beta” or “NEO Beta home robot”).
- Soft-body, sweatsuit aesthetic emphasized in launch materials.

### UBTech / UWorld — U1 Series (June–July 2026, still active)
- Full-size ultra-bionic companion robots: U1 Lite (semi-torso), U1 Pro, U1 Ultra.
- **88 degrees of freedom**, dual-pivot biomimetic cervical spine (~90% of basic human movements).
- Soft synthetic/silicone skin, facial expressions + lip sync in ~20 ms.
- Emotional AI: identifies 20+ emotional states (~90% claimed accuracy).
- **Agent Memory OS** for long-term memory of people, preferences, and routines.
- Pricing starts ~¥119,800 (~$17,650) for base; higher for Ultra.
- 13,000+ pre-orders reported; first deliveries targeted for September 2026.
- Marketed as lifelong loyal companions (especially for singles and older adults).
- Male and female models shown (female ~168 cm / 35 kg).

**Media:**
- Launch photos: woman interacting with / touching the face of U1 robots (AFP / China Daily coverage, June 30, 2026).
- Close-up videos from UBTECH official channel: “UWORLD Ultra-Bionic Humanoid Robot U1 Pro — Up Close” and WRC 2026 reaction clips.
- Highly detailed silicone skin, manicured nails, and expressive eyes featured heavily.

### Realbotix — Aria & Melody
- **Aria**: Most lifelike companion currently shipping (~$125,000).
- **Melody**: Portable open-source sister model (~$95,000, built to order).
- Modular faces (magnetic/RFID), high facial motor count, conversational AI (cloud or on-device).

**Media:**
- Product photography and conversation demos on Realbotix / gfs.ai.
- CES and booth photos showing Aria in conversation.

### Other Notable Mentions
- **XPeng IRON**: Raised $900M+ (Aug 2026) at ~$6.3B valuation. 76 DoF body + 21 DoF per hand, synthetic skin, on-device Turing chips (2,250 TOPS). Mass production targeted end of 2026; deliveries 2027. More general-purpose / industrial-leaning but human-like appearance.
- Broader market: Global humanoid shipments strong in H1 2026 (AgiBot & Unitree leading volume). Consumer companion segment still early but accelerating with emotional AI and soft skins.

**Media:**
- Wikimedia / public event photos of IRON (e.g., Auto Guangzhou 2025 and later mall appearances).
- Stage demos where XPeng cut open the leg to prove it is a robot (not a person in a suit).
